Staff

For logistics and administrative questions, please email cs61b@berkeley.edu. Access to these emails is denoted by the Staff Email Access tag. Future instructors and head TAs may also be able to read emails here, but we can delete emails upon request.

Staff with the DSP tag can see the DSP letter of accommodations (knowing whether or not a student is DSP does not count as DSP access).

Staff with the STUDENT SUPPORT tag can see student support meeting notes, extenuating circumstances form responses, and see/respond to emails for cs61b-student-support@berkeley.edu.
